# Next earnings monitor - a pre-results scenario checklist

## Purpose

This monitor turns NVIDIA's published FY2027 Q3 revenue guidance into a
repeatable pre-results framework. It is deliberately a scenario tool, not a
forecast, price target or investment recommendation.

## Starting point

NVIDIA guided FY2027 Q3 revenue to **USD 108.0bn, plus or minus 2%**. That
creates a company guidance range of **USD 105.84bn to USD 110.16bn**. NVIDIA's
outlook did not assume any Data Center compute revenue from China.

## How to interpret a reported revenue outcome

| Illustrative outcome | Immediate analytical question | What cannot be inferred automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Below USD 105.84bn | Did demand, supply, product timing or a geographic assumption change? | A negative share-price reaction or a permanent deterioration in the business. |
| Within the range | Does the next-quarter guide sustain the broader growth trajectory? | That the market will view a guided outcome as sufficient. |
| Above USD 110.16bn | Is the magnitude and quality of the upside durable, and does guidance extend it? | A consensus beat or a positive share-price reaction. |

## Checklist for the result and call

1. **Revenue and guide:** Is the result inside or outside the own-guidance
   range, and how does the next range change the trajectory?
2. **Data Center demand:** Does commentary point to wider customer demand or
   greater concentration?
3. **Margins and platform transition:** Is revenue growth accompanied by an
   outlook that supports the underlying economics?
4. **China assumption:** Does management continue to exclude Data Center
   compute revenue from China, quantify a change or identify a new constraint?
5. **Hyperscaler funding:** Do reported cash flow and capex disclosures still
   support the investment cycle described in this project?

## Limitation

The repository does not include a dated third-party consensus dataset. It
therefore distinguishes only between a result and NVIDIA's own prior guidance,
not between a result and external consensus expectations.
