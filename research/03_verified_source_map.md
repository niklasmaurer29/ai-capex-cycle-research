# Verified source map - phase 1

The first research phase establishes where every issuer publishes its official
financial material. These portals have been checked directly. No numerical
claim is made below; the next phase selects the exact fiscal-period documents
and records each figure with its page reference.

| Issuer | Official source portal | Intended evidence |
|---|---|---|
| NVIDIA | [Annual Reports and Proxies](https://investor.nvidia.com/financial-info/annual-reports-and-proxies/default.aspx) | Form 10-K, data-centre revenue, profitability, cash flow and risk disclosures |
| Microsoft | [Annual Reports](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/annualreports) | Form 10-K, property and equipment additions, cash flow and cloud commentary |
| Alphabet | [Investor Relations](https://abc.xyz/investor/) | Form 10-K, capital expenditure, cash flow and earnings releases |
| Amazon | [Annual Reports, Proxies and Shareholder Letters](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/annual-reports-proxies-and-shareholder-letters/default.aspx) | Form 10-K, property and equipment additions, cash flow and shareholder-letter commentary |
| Meta | [Financials](https://investor.atmeta.com/financials/default.aspx) | Form 10-K, capital expenditure, cash flow and earnings releases |

## Selection rule for the next phase

For each company, the fact base will identify the precise report title,
fiscal-year end and page reference before entering a numerical value. Calendar
year labels are not assumed to equal each other: NVIDIA's fiscal calendar and
the hyperscalers' reporting calendars must remain visible in the final table.

## What the first data extraction will capture

1. The issuer's reported capex or property-and-equipment measure, exactly as
   defined in its report.
2. Revenue, operating-profit and free-cash-flow evidence needed to assess the
   scale and funding of investment.
3. NVIDIA data-centre revenue and consolidated cash-generation evidence.
4. The source of each statement, including whether it is historical reporting
   or forward guidance.
